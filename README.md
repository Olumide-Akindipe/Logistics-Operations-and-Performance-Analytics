# Logistics Operations Analytics
### Power BI Business Intelligence & Operational Performance

## Project Overview

This project explores how business intelligence and operational analytics can support decision-making in logistics operations. This report analyzes interconnected operational data across revenue, routes, fleet utilization, maintenance, deliveries, and driver safety.

## Business Questions

- How are financial performance and service reliability trending?
- Which routes and lanes generate the strongest and weakest margins?
- How do vehicle age, maintenance expenditure, and downtime relate?
- How can driver performance and safety be evaluated fairly?
- What relationships between driver experience and fleet characteristics merit further investigation?

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Executive Performance Overview
Summarizes key financial and operational indicators, including revenue, operating profit, profit margin, on-time delivery, and safety incidents.

### 2. Route Coverage & Profitability
Examines lane profitability and the relationship between revenue and service reliability to identify areas for further operational review.

### 3. Fleet Productivity & Maintenance
Analyzes fleet utilization, fuel efficiency, maintenance expenditure, vehicle age, and downtime.

### 4. Driver Performance, Safety & Workforce Risk
Evaluates driver performance and safety, using exposure-adjusted measures where appropriate.

### 5. Driver Experience & Fleet Performance Analytics
Explores observed relationships between driver tenure, vehicle characteristics, safety risk, and maintenance burden.

## Selected Findings

- Revenue was approximately $262.5 million, with operating profit of approximately $161.2 million.
- On-time performance was approximately 33.3%, highlighting a service reliability improvement opportunity.
- Older vehicles showed a substantially higher maintenance and downtime burden in the analyzed data.
- Incident rates varied across driver tenure groups, reinforcing the value of exposure-adjusted safety analysis.

These findings describe patterns in the available dataset and do not, by themselves, establish causation.
